## Post Structure

### Hook (1–2 sentences)

{A surprising result, a counterintuitive finding, or a question that stops the scroll. Lead with the most interesting thing you learned.}

### Problem Statement (2–3 sentences)

{What challenge were you solving? Frame it as a real B2B industrial problem — make the reader nod in recognition. Be specific: numbers, constraints, domain details.}

### Solution / Insight (3–5 sentences)

{What did you build or discover? Walk through the approach at a technical-but-accessible level. Use concrete examples. Name the tools/techniques but explain why you chose them.}

### Metric / Proof (1–2 sentences)

{Show a measurable result. Accuracy, latency, coverage, before/after comparison. Numbers build credibility.}

### Open Question / CTA (1–2 sentences)

{End with a genuine question or invitation to discuss. Not a sales pitch — a conversation starter. Ask something you're actually curious about or where you'd value input.}

---

## Formatting Checklist

- [ ] Total length: ~150–250 words
- [ ] Written in English
- [ ] No past client names or confidential information
- [ ] All data comes from this repo's code, synthetic data, and metrics
- [ ] Professional experience framed as domain expertise, not client work
- [ ] Includes at least one concrete number or metric
- [ ] Ends with a question or conversation starter
- [ ] No corporate buzzwords — keep it authentic and conversational
- [ ] Hashtags (3–5 max): #BuildInPublic #AI #{relevant_topic}
